Dashboard Features

Welcome to the Admin Dashboard. Here you can manage all aspects of the Student Management System.

1. Student Management
  • View All Students: List of all students with options to edit, view details, or delete.
  • Add New Student: A form to add new students with their details like roll number, class, and parent name.
  • Student Reports: Generate reports for students based on their attendance and grades.
2. Teacher Management
  • View All Teachers: List of all teachers with their details like department, employee ID, and joining date.
  • Assign Subjects: Assign subjects or courses to teachers.
  • Add New Teacher: A form to add new teachers with their department and employee ID.
3. Assignment Management
  • View All Assignments: Manage assignments given to students, including due dates and assigned teachers.
  • Create Assignment: Add new assignments with a title, description, and due date.
  • Assignment Grading: Enable teachers to grade assignments and track student progress.
4. Attendance Tracking
  • Daily Attendance: Record and manage daily attendance for students.
  • Attendance Reports: Generate attendance reports for students over time.
  • Set Attendance Rules: Configure rules for absences.
5. Student-Teacher Interaction
  • Teacher Assignments: View and manage assignments for each teacher.
  • Feedback Management: Allow feedback exchange between students and teachers.
  • Parent Communication: Communicate with parents about student performance.
6. Dashboard Analytics
  • Total Students & Teachers: View real-time data on the number of students, teachers, and assignments.
  • Performance Metrics: Track student and teacher performance through dashboards and charts.
  • Upcoming Deadlines: Display upcoming assignment and exam deadlines.
7. User Roles & Permissions
  • Role Management: Assign roles like admin, teacher, or student and manage permissions.
  • Activity Logs: View logs of actions performed by users on the dashboard.
8. Settings
  • School Year Management: Set the current academic year.
  • Email & Notification Settings: Configure notifications for events like new assignments or feedback.
  • Security Settings: Manage password policies and authentication settings.
